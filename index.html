Help

You have
—

Best at the top. Five cards play — your two plus the five on the table.

The percentage is how often seven cards make that hand, if every hand ran to the river. Most fold long before, so it is not your chance of winning with one. Hover a percentage for the odds the long way round.

The shape of a hand

You get two cards nobody else sees. Five more are dealt face up in the middle, in three goes — three, then one, then one. Your hand is the best five out of those seven, and you can use both, one or neither of your own.

Betting

Two players post blinds to start, so there is always something to play for. Then you act in turn, and again after each new card.

  • Fold — give up the hand and anything you put in.
  • Check — stay in without paying, if nobody has bet.
  • Call — match the current bet.
  • Bet / Raise — put more in and make everyone else match it.

Sizing a bet

The slider sets the amount. Min is the smallest raise allowed, Pot matches what is already in the middle, and All in pushes everything you have. A bigger bet makes it cost more to stay in against you.

Without touching the mouse

F fold · C check or call · R bet or raise

Winning

Everyone else folding wins it there and then, and you never have to show. Otherwise the best hand takes the pot, split evenly on a tie. Bet more than someone has and they are all in for what they hold — the rest goes into a side pot they cannot win.
